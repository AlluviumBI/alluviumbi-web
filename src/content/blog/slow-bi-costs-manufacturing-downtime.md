In high-volume manufacturing, speed isn’t just about production—it’s about insight. Every delay in uncovering the *why* behind a quality issue or a machine stoppage translates into escalating scrap, missed quotas, and lost revenue.

**Yet too many BI systems can’t keep up.**

### The Real Cost of a Slow Dashboard

When a key metric spikes—like a defect rate or cycle time—you need immediate visibility. In laggy systems, that alert might arrive after **tens of thousands of units** have already passed through the line. Some of the true business costs:
- **Delayed Root Cause Analysis:** Problems aren’t isolated fast enough. That increases the time to resolution.
- **Cascading Quality Issues:** Undetected anomalies affect downstream steps, multiplying rework.
- **Operational Downtime:** Waiting on insights often means waiting on fixes. And waiting costs money.
- **Reduced Trust in Data:** When teams doubt the system’s responsiveness, they stop using it proactively.

### Why Power BI Struggles in These Environments

Power BI is powerful—but not magic. Manufacturing data typically includes:
- **Streaming inputs** from sensors and production logs
- **High cardinality** (e.g., one row per unit or cycle)
- **Large volumes** of daily data across shifts, lines, and plants

Out-of-the-box Power BI models can’t handle that kind of weight without tuning. Poor data model design, inefficient DAX, and over-reliance on import mode lead to **slow visuals, frequent timeouts, and frustrated teams**.
