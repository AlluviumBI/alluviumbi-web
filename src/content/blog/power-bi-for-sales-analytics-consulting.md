## Why Sales Teams Struggle to Use Data

Even with CRMs in place, most sales teams deal with:
- Multiple reports from different platforms (CRM, ERP, spreadsheets)
- Outdated or inconsistent KPIs
- No single source of truth for pipeline, targets, or territory performance

As a result, leaders ask:

> “Why do our numbers look different in every report?”
 “Which deals are actually real?”
 “Where are we off pace—and why?”

## What Power BI Brings to Sales Analytics

Power BI connects sales data sources and transforms them into clear, interactive dashboards that help leaders:
- Monitor pipelines and quotas in real time
- Drill into stalled deals by rep, product, or region
- Track conversion rates, sales velocity, and cycle length
- Align CRM data with financial targets for better forecasting

Used correctly, Power BI is more than a reporting tool—it becomes a sales management system.
