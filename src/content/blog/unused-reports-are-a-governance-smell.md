Zero views is a signal. Refreshing ghosts is how capacity and trust both leak.

One quiet page can be a product miss. An estate of quiet pages is a governance miss. The usage log is trying to tell you the certified layer never happened, the attic never emptied, and refresh is still treating everything as live.

![Black-and-white unused wooden beehive boxes standing in a field](/blog/unused-reports-are-a-governance-smell-hero.jpg)

## This is the estate, not the empty Tuesday, not the funeral

[Nobody opens the dashboard](/blog/nobody-opens-the-dashboard) is one report that lost the meeting: wrong grain, slow, untrusted, no owner. Fix that page or kill it. Do not retread adoption of a single brochure here.

[How to retire a dashboard](/blog/how-to-retire-a-dashboard) is the ritual for one file: owner, replacement, date, announcement. You will use that ritual. This piece is when to notice the *smell* across the tenant, and the cleanup list that is not a one-off funeral.

[Dashboard sprawl is a tax](/blog/dashboard-sprawl-is-a-tax) is the inventory slogan. This post is the signal that slogan missed: usage near zero, refresh still on, endorsement still looking official. Ghosts with a schedule.

[Judgment:] if you cannot say which reports the company actually runs, you do not have governance. You have hosting.

## What the smell actually is

Usage is not a vanity metric. It is which products participate in decisions.

A handful of analyst clicks is not a meeting. A report that only the author opens is a working paper that escaped. A certified badge on a file nobody has opened in a quarter is a lying label.

Ghosts cost even when seats are assigned: [unused licenses](/blog/unused-power-bi-licenses) is the invoice shape. This is the workspace shape. You can have full seats and still refresh a museum.

The smell shows up together:

**Refresh of the unviewed.** Gateways, capacity, 6 a.m. collisions. The morning window is eaten by jobs nobody needed: [the 7 a.m. surprise](/blog/gateway-refresh-7am-surprise).

**Endorsement without an audience.** Promoted and certified mean “safe” only if people can find the live one. A wall of badges is camouflage.

**Twins of the live pack.** The certified app is quiet because the old name still works. Adoption failed at the front door, estate-wide.

**No owner on the long tail.** Authors left. Bookmarks remain. Access that was fine for a project is not fine two years later. See [hidden costs of Power BI governance](https://www.alluviumbi.com/blog/the-hidden-costs-of-poor-power-bi-governance-and-how-to-fix-it).

Pull the usage you have. Ask the EA who builds the pack. If both are quiet on a file, that is data. It is not a reason to keep the schedule “just in case.”

## What an unviewed estate costs

1. **Capacity leaks on ghosts.** Unused reports still refresh, still fail, still page someone at dawn. You buy headroom to feed the attic. [Premium capacity is not a strategy](/blog/premium-capacity-is-not-a-strategy). Turning jobs off is the cheaper capacity plan.

2. **Trust leaks from the label.** If “certified” includes files nobody uses, the word means nothing. People keep the emailed workbook. The next certified page inherits the shrug.

3. **Risk sits where nobody looks.** Old RLS, stale vendor lists, columns that should have died with a project. Review follows usage. Unviewed is unreviewed.

4. **The live product stays buried.** Navigation is a scavenger hunt. The steward’s app looks empty because thirty doors still open. Sprawl was the tax. Ghosts are how the tax compounds after the build.

5. **Analysts rebuild instead of pointing.** Copying feels safer than trusting a quiet catalog. Drift continues. You pay to explain two truths: [why reports show different numbers](/blog/why-power-bi-reports-show-different-numbers).

6. **Roadmaps get funded on fiction.** Leaders add pages and tools on top of an unused foundation. The estate looks busy in a workspace list. The meetings never moved.

No invented usage percentages. Open activity. If meeting owners are not there, the file failed—even if a handful of clicks still land.

## The cleanup list (estate level)

Do this as a pass, then keep it as hygiene. Funerals still use the ritual. The pass is how you choose the queue.

1. **Inventory with usage, not with pride.** Workspace, report, last viewed by a consumer (not the author), refresh on or off, endorsement, named owner. No owner plus no views is the first pile. Author-only views is the second. Real usage without an owner is a rescue, not a funeral.

2. **Split the piles in writing.** *Ghosts:* near-zero consumer use for a cycle, refresh still on. *Twins:* usage exists but duplicates a certified decision. *Live:* the meeting actually opens it. Only live stays in the app people are told to use. Ghosts and twins get dates.

3. **Turn refresh off before you argue taste.** For ghosts, the first save is the morning window. A dark report that still refreshes is the smell. Disable the schedule. Keep the file visible with a banner if you must. Capacity returns before the political fight.

4. **Strip lying labels.** Remove certified and promoted from anything that is not live and owned. Endorsement is a promise. Unused is a broken promise. Recertify only the thin layer: [certified models vs the wild west](/blog/certified-datasets-vs-wild-west).

5. **Queue funerals, do not declare a mass grave.** Each ghost still needs owner, replacement or “decision dead,” and a date: the ritual post. Batch announcements by domain. Do not delete a tenant on a Friday. Do not recertify everything as a substitute for sunset.

6. **Close the hose.** Cleanup without intake is a season. Stop commissioning a page per question. Standing executive asks go into the model: [questions that should never be ad-hoc](/blog/questions-that-should-never-be-adhoc). Review usage monthly like incidents. A copy with a new name is a resurrection. Send it through commissioning, not through nostalgia.

Change leadership is how the live door becomes default: [data project management](https://www.alluviumbi.com/data-project-management-change-leadership). If nobody can say which decisions the estate serves, you need a [roadmap](/analytics-ai-strategy-roadmap) before a purge. A [Quickstart](/power-bi-quickstart) can still be the one live loop you point at while the attic darkens. Ongoing conscience is [managed advisory](/managed-advisory-retainer).

## What good looks like

You can name the reports the ELT actually runs. It is a short list.

Refresh jobs match that list plus a labeled sandbox with no endorsement.

Certified means used and owned. Unused means dark, then gone.

The attic does not refill every quarter.

## Frequently asked questions

**What if something unused is needed once a year?**
Then it is not a daily product. Label it, refresh on a calendar that matches the use, or run it as a working paper with an owner. Do not keep a daily job for an annual question.

**Should we delete everything with zero views this month?**
No. Prove neglect or replacement, then use the ritual. Silent takedown is how zombies come back by Monday.

**Is low usage always a fail?**
For an executive pack, yes. For a steward’s diagnostic page, maybe not. The smell is the *estate* of unviewed, unowned, still-refreshing files wearing a badge. One quiet diagnostic is not the signal.
