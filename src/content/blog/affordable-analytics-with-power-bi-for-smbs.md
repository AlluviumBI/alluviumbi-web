## Small Business, Real Data, Limited Budget
If you run a small or midsize business and want to make sense of your data, you have probably hit the same wall as everyone else. The tools that promise insight cost too much or take too long to set up.

Platforms like Tableau, Looker, and Domo sound impressive until you see the price tag and the setup effort. You do not need to overspend or overbuild to get clear, reliable numbers. That is where Power BI and Alluvium come in.

**Why Power BI Makes Sense for SMBs**

Power BI is Microsoft’s modern analytics platform, built for real businesses with real budgets. You get advanced dashboards, interactive visuals, natural language Q&A, and strong security on a platform that works with the tools you already use. Depending on your existing Microsoft subscriptions, Power BI may already be included.

If your team lives in Microsoft 365, Excel, Teams, or Outlook, Power BI fits right in. It feels familiar, and it grows with the business.

**How Alluvium Helps You Get the Most Out of Power BI**

Alluvium helps small and midsize companies get useful reporting in place quickly. Whether you are new to reporting or already working with data, we start where you are and build from there. In practice that means:

- **Quick setup** with templates and reporting frameworks that get you running fast
- **Plain language**, with clear dashboards that help your team make better decisions
- **Custom connectors** to pull in data from HubSpot, Salesforce, QuickBooks, Snowflake, and more
- **Training and support** so your team uses the reports instead of admiring them

We stay after the first report ships. We help your team build skill and set up reporting that runs on its own and scales as you grow.

**Bottom Line: Better Decisions Shouldn’t Break the Bank**

You should not have to choose between running lean and getting the numbers you need. Power BI and Alluvium give growing businesses strong analytics without the complexity or cost of an enterprise platform.
